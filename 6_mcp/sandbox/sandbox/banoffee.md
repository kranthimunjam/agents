# Traditional Banoffee Pie

Banoffee pie is a quintessential English dessert made from bananas, cream, caramel (or dulce de leche), and a buttery biscuit base. This classic recipe delivers an irresistible combination of textures and flavors.

## Ingredients

### Crust
* **Graham cracker crumbs** (or traditional English Digestive biscuits) – 1 and 1/2 cups (150g)
* **Unsalted butter** – 6 tablespoons (85g), melted
* **Granulated sugar** – 2 tablespoons (optional, for extra sweetness)

### Filling & Toppings
* **Dulce de leche** or thick caramel sauce – 1 can (approx. 13.4 oz / 380g to 400g) *(Note: You can also boil an unopened can of sweetened condensed milk for 3 hours)*
* **Ripe bananas** – 2 to 3 medium bananas, sliced
* **Heavy whipping cream** – 1 cup (240ml), cold
* **Confectioners' sugar** (powdered sugar) – 2 tablespoons
* **Pure vanilla extract** – 1 teaspoon
* **Garnish**: Grated dark chocolate, cocoa powder, or toffee bits

---

## Instructions

### 1. Make the Crust
1. Combine the graham cracker (or digestive biscuit) crumbs, melted butter, and granulated sugar in a medium bowl until the mixture resembles wet sand.
2. Pour the mixture into a 9-inch pie dish. Press it firmly and evenly down into the bottom and up the sides of the dish.
3. Chill the crust in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to set, or bake at 350°F (175°C) for 8–10 minutes if you prefer a baked, crunchier crust (let cool completely before filling).

### 2. Add the Caramel Layer
1. Spread the dulce de leche or thick caramel sauce evenly over the chilled or cooled biscuit crust. 
2. Smooth it out with a spatula.

### 3. Layer the Bananas
1. Slice the bananas into even rounds (about 1/2-inch thick).
2. Arrange the banana slices tightly over the caramel layer. You can do a single dense layer or double layer depending on how fruity you like it!

### 4. Whip the Cream & Assemble
1. In a large cold bowl, whip the heavy whipping cream, confectioners' sugar, and vanilla extract together using an electric mixer on medium-high speed until stiff peaks form (about 3–4 minutes).
2. Spoon or pipe the whipped cream generously over the top of the bananas, covering the pie completely.

### 5. Chill and Garnish
1. Refrigerate the pie for at least 2 to 3 hours (or up to overnight) to allow the flavors to meld and the pie to firm up.
2. Just before serving, garnish the top with grated dark chocolate, a dusting of cocoa powder, or chopped toffee bits.

---

## Notes & Tips
* **Preventing browning**: The bananas are sealed between the caramel and whipped cream, which naturally helps prevent them from turning brown quickly. However, slicing them right before assembly is best.
* **Make-Ahead**: You can prepare this pie a day in advance and keep it stored in the refrigerator.
